Develop the idea

This is one possible answer, not the only correct one. Different reasons and experiences are fine if they answer the question.

Self-check after you answer

Keys: ← → move · Space reveal or hide the model · H hint · 1–4 jump to a step

Teaching cue: Let the student finish before you reveal anything. In Develop the idea, use only the steps that add something new, and skip a step if the student's first sentence already covers it. In the interview questions, the target structure is welcome but never required: the goal is a clear, relevant answer, not a longer one.
Quick check for any answer
  • Does it answer the actual question?
  • Does each sentence add new information, or does it repeat the last one?
  • Is the cause → effect link clear?
  • Is the example realistic and relevant?
  • Are the verb forms and subject–verb agreement correct?

Keys: ← → move · Space reveal or hide the full chain · H guiding questions

Oral prompts: Explanation = How or why does the reason work? Example = What specific person or situation shows it? Consequence = So what changes? Each step should add something new. Hide the models and ask for the full chain again. The student can give different valid examples.
