VOCABULARY LAB / SPEAKING

Use EN–/EM– verbs in Speaking

Practise expressing your ideas while using academic vocabulary accurately. Each question gives you one target word and shows you how to build a sentence with it.

How to practise

1. Read the question and the highlighted target word.

2. Use that word at least once in your spoken answer. Adapt its form if needed. Aim for a clear answer with a reason or example, not a memorized sentence.

3. After answering, reveal the example. Check whether you used the word naturally and with the correct grammar.

These are guided vocabulary-production questions inspired by TOEFL Take an Interview. They are not a strict exam simulation because real test questions do not tell you which word to use.
